Three big bets

Gate
Free percentile, exact score for an email, on one screen

I chose one merged screen over a teaser plus a signup, assuming each extra tap loses visitors. The free percentile gives value before the ask. Cost: no standalone score reveal.

Moves gate → signup. Part 2, variant 3 tests it.

Length
12 questions, not 20

I assumed, without measuring, that completion falls as length grows. Twelve items still span all four reasoning types. Cost: a noisier score, so every score is labelled an estimate.

Moves quiz start → complete. Part 2, variants 1–2 target this step.

Continuity
The hero is the real Question 1, and the ads repeat the landing

A visitor who clicked “What’s your IQ?” sees the same words and the quiz itself at once. A “Free IQ Test” tag stays for Search match. Cost: ads must change whenever the landing does.

Moves landing → quiz start.

Other decisions

No visible timer in control. A countdown adds anxiety for cold traffic; Part 2 tests it.
Google + email signup. One tap removes typing. Google is a labelled prototype demo; production uses Google Identity Services.
“An estimated X% of people.” There is no real test-taker population yet, so I claim no measured data.
“Challenge a Friend” after signup. A growth loop that brings the next visitor in, not a CVR lever.
Honest by construction. No fake stats or reviews; every colour pair is contrast-checked.

One reversal: my first hypothesis had an undisclosed mock Google button. I cut it, then restored it as a labelled demo.

How I built it

  • I designed the funnel in Claude Design and built it in Claude Code with a custom toolkit: 5 skills (quiz builder, CRO audit, A/B designer, creative builder, automated QA) and 5 agents (4 visitor personas, 1 hiring-manager reviewer).
  • The loop: build → automated QA at 390 and 1440px → CRO audit and persona reviews → fix → re-verify.
  • AI caught a real issue. The mobile persona quit when answer options ran off a 390×664 screen. Screenshots confirmed it on questions 3 and 7; after my layout fix, all 4 options fit on screen.
  • I overrode the AI. Its first plan hid the speed bonus from visitors. I required it to be disclosed, so the quiz says “speed counts a little.”
